Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ECRA/ESA licence number and why is it important?

In Ontario, electrical work must be performed by a licensed electrical contractor. ECRA/ESA #7016330 certifies that A-Wire Electrical Contracting Inc. is licensed by the Electrical Safety Authority, fully insured, and complies with all Ontario Electrical Safety Code standards.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency electrical repairs?

Yes! We provide round-the-clock 24/7 emergency service for urgent electrical issues such as sudden power outages, burning smells from outlets or panels, buzzing breakers, or storm damage.

How long does a 200-Amp panel upgrade take?

A standard 200-Amp main panel upgrade typically takes 6 to 8 hours to complete. We coordinate power shutoff with Hydro and schedule ESA inspection to ensure minimal downtime for your home.

Are free estimates provided before work begins?

Absolutely. We provide transparent upfront quotes with detailed breakdowns before starting any work. You can request a quote online or call us at 905 955-5455.
