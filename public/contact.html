Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency electrical service?

Yes! We provide round-the-clock 24/7 emergency electrical service across Mississauga, Oakville, Brampton, Toronto, and Milton. If you experience a sudden power outage, burning odor from an outlet, or a flickering breaker panel at night, call 905 955-5455 immediately.

Why should I upgrade my panel to 200 Amps?

Older homes with 60A or 100A electrical panels often struggle to power modern high-draw appliances like Level 2 EV chargers, central air conditioning, induction cooktops, and hot tubs. Upgrading to a 200A panel prevents tripped breakers, enhances home safety, and increases property resale value.

Are you licensed by the Electrical Safety Authority (ESA)?

Absolutely. A-Wire Electrical Contracting Inc. holds ECRA/ESA License # 7016330. All our master electricians are fully licensed, insured, and file official ESA permits and inspections for your peace of mind.

How long does a Level 2 EV Charger installation take?

Most residential EV charger installations take between 2 to 4 hours depending on the distance between your electrical panel and garage mounting location. We handle load calculation, conduit installation, and testing.
